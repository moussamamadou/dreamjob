# Sand on Glass: style guide

A portable visual style for video, thumbnails, covers and Shorts. It works in any project: swap the palette and the props, keep the rules.

**In one line:** the story is drawn live in dark sand on a glowing lightbox, the way sand-animation performers work, with soft grainy silhouettes.

**The twist:** Impermanence. Every scene is wiped away by a hand to make the next, like a career that leaves no trace.

**Why it is face-free:** Sand figures are soft silhouettes with no features. Consistency comes from the outline (hat, coat, posture), which is easy to keep.

**Where it comes from:** Sand animation by Caroline Leaf (*The Owl Who Married a Goose*, 1974) and live sand-art performance. *References from memory.* Take the technique, never copy a specific design.

---

## 1. Core rules

1. Dark sand on warm backlight only.
2. Figures are outlines and masses; no features ever.
3. Each character has one silhouette: hat, coat, build.
4. Grains and finger lines stay visible.
5. Scenes change by a sweep of sand, a hand wiping, or sand pouring in.
6. One accent: a red glass bead, the only colour in the frame.
7. Death: the figure is slowly brushed away into the ground. Told straight.

## 2. Palette

| Role | Share | Default (Dream Job) |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | 40 to 60% | cream `#EFE3C8` or the set colour |
| Main colours | 20 to 35% | mustard `#D9A23A`, teal `#2F7C78` |
| Ink | 10 to 20% | `#24211D` |
| Accent | under 5% | red `#C23A2B`, once per frame |

**Greyscale check:** red and teal are the same grey in the default palette, so the accent never sits on teal. Other channels keep the structure: one ground, two mid-tones, an ink, one hot accent.

## 3. Cast

Case study: his hat and coat outline. Others: different hat outlines. Crowds: a band of sand with a row of hat tops.

Make one reference sheet per recurring character (front, side, back, on a plain background) and use it as the image reference for every later shot.

## 4. Composition and type

- One or two characters per frame, one clear action.
- Leave one clean area for live text in thumbnails and title cards.
- All text, stamps and the career file are added in Remotion, never generated.

## 5. Motion

**In MiniMax:** Excellent and on-theme. Sand pouring, a figure walking, a wipe to the next scene, flickering backlight. Morphing is the style here, so MiniMax drift looks intended.

General MiniMax rules: one motion per clip, describe camera and motion only, no text in the frame, and QA the contact sheet for faces appearing, extra limbs and duplicated objects. Remotion handles cuts, text, stamps and counters.

## 6. Prompt template

```
Sand animation on a glowing lightbox, seen from above. Dark fine sand pushed by fingers into a scene: [scene and figure with hat outline], soft-edged dark sand shapes against warm amber backlight, finger lines and grains visible, no facial features. One small red glass bead as [accent]. No text.
```

## 7. When to use it

**Strong for:** Transitions, memories, the exit interview, origin stories, biography and history channels, music videos.

**Weak for:** Explaining numbers or anything that needs detail. Pair with Isotype Ledger.

## 8. Checklist

- [ ] No face, eyes or mouth anywhere.
- [ ] Each character matches his reference sheet.
- [ ] One accent, not on teal.
- [ ] No text in the art.
- [ ] Deaths and victims told straight, never as a gag.
- [ ] Reads at 168 px wide (thumbnail size).
