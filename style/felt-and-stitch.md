# Felt and Stitch: style guide

A portable visual style for video, thumbnails, covers and Shorts. It works in any project: swap the palette and the props, keep the rules.

**In one line:** handmade felt dolls with plain round felt heads, blanket-stitched clothes and felt sets, shot like cosy stop-motion.

**The twist:** The coziest possible look for the hardest stories: the softness makes the straight-told facts land harder.

**Why it is face-free:** Felt dolls are made without faces here: plain round heads. Each character is a felt costume and a hat, and textile toys stay recognisable shot to shot.

**Where it comes from:** Handmade felt and wool stop-motion puppetry and mid-century European children's television puppet films. *References from memory.* Take the technique, never copy a specific design.

---

## 1. Core rules

1. Plain round felt heads, no eyes, no mouth, no embroidery on the face.
2. Visible stitches, fibres and seams.
3. Each character has a felt costume and a felt hat, fixed for the series.
4. Sets are felt and wood, at toy scale, with warm light and shallow focus.
5. Money, guns and objects are felt too: soft versions of hard things.
6. One accent: a red felt band or object.
7. Death: the doll is laid down and covered with a felt blanket. Told straight; no felt blood ever.

## 2. Palette

| Role | Share | Default (Dream Job) |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | 40 to 60% | cream `#EFE3C8` or the set colour |
| Main colours | 20 to 35% | mustard `#D9A23A`, teal `#2F7C78` |
| Ink | 10 to 20% | `#24211D` |
| Accent | under 5% | red `#C23A2B`, once per frame |

**Greyscale check:** red and teal are the same grey in the default palette, so the accent never sits on teal. Other channels keep the structure: one ground, two mid-tones, an ink, one hot accent.

## 3. Cast

Case study: felt suit and fedora with a red band. Crew: same hat, darker felt. Police: felt cap. Bank tellers and public: plain cream heads, simple felt clothes.

Make one reference sheet per recurring character (front, side, back, on a plain background) and use it as the image reference for every later shot.

## 4. Composition and type

- One or two characters per frame, one clear action.
- Leave one clean area for live text in thumbnails and title cards.
- All text, stamps and the career file are added in Remotion, never generated.

## 5. Motion

**In MiniMax:** Very good. Small stop-motion moves: handing over notes, turning, walking a few steps. Risk: models may stitch on eyes; prompt 'no eyes, no mouth' and QA every clip.

General MiniMax rules: one motion per clip, describe camera and motion only, no text in the frame, and QA the contact sheet for faces appearing, extra limbs and duplicated objects. Remotion handles cuts, text, stamps and counters.

## 6. Prompt template

```
Handmade felt stop-motion set. Faceless felt doll figures with plain round felt heads and no eyes or mouth, blanket-stitched edges, [case study in felt costume and hat with red band] [action] [location in felt]. Cosy warm light, visible fibres and stitches, shallow depth of field. Palette: cream #EFE3C8, mustard #D9A23A, teal #2F7C78, ink #24211D, red #C23A2B. No faces, no text.
```

## 7. When to use it

**Strong for:** Bank robbers, con artists, dark history for a broad audience, kids-adjacent channels (keep Dream Job separate from the kids' books).

**Weak for:** Very violent scenes; keep them off screen.

## 8. Checklist

- [ ] No face, eyes or mouth anywhere.
- [ ] Each character matches his reference sheet.
- [ ] One accent, not on teal.
- [ ] No text in the art.
- [ ] Deaths and victims told straight, never as a gag.
- [ ] Reads at 168 px wide (thumbnail size).
