# Neon Night: style guide

A portable visual style for night scenes, crime, cities, nightlife and atmosphere, in video, thumbnails and Shorts. It works in any project: swap the palette, keep the rules.

**In one line:** the city at night told through its signs: neon shapes, rain, wet reflections, and lone figures seen as dark shapes from behind or far away.

**Why it is face-free:** at night, people are ink shapes under hats and umbrellas, lit from behind by signs. The signs, not the people, carry the story: a cocktail glass for a speakeasy, a dollar sign for a bank, a star for a club. Signs are simple graphic shapes that stay consistent.

**Where it comes from:** mid-century flat illustration of city nights, film-noir street compositions, and 1940s and 1950s neon signage and matchbook graphics. *References from memory.*

---

## 1. Core rules

1. **Night only.** Ink sky, teal shadows, the light comes from signs.
2. **Signs are wordless shapes.** A glass, an arrow, a star, a crown, a dollar sign. Any words are added in Remotion, never generated.
3. **Figures are dark shapes.** From behind, in profile under a hat brim, or far away. Never lit from the front.
4. **Wet ground doubles everything.** Every sign has a reflection in the street.
5. **Verticals dominate.** Tall buildings, tall signs, rain lines.
6. **One sign in the accent colour** per frame: the one the story is about.

## 2. Palette

| Role | Share | Default (Dream Job) |
|---|---|---|
| Night | 40 to 60% | ink `#24211D` |
| Buildings and wet street | 20 to 35% | teal `#2F7C78` |
| Light | 10 to 20% | mustard `#D9A23A`, cream `#EFE3C8` |
| Accent sign | under 5% | red `#C23A2B` |

## 3. Sign vocabulary (reusable)

Cocktail glass (speakeasy, Prohibition), dollar or coin (bank, money), star (club, fame), arrow (direction, choice), crown (boss), dice (gambling), anchor (port), horseshoe (luck), key (hotel, prison), clock (time running out).

## 4. Composition and type

- Street perspective is allowed, but flat colour planes, no realistic lighting.
- One figure, small, in the lower third; the signs fill the upper two thirds.
- Thumbnails: one accent sign and one figure; three words added in a neon-tube style in Remotion.

## 5. Motion

- **In MiniMax: excellent.** Neon flicker, rain, ripples in puddles, a figure walking away, a car's headlights passing. One motion per clip.
- Prompt: "the red arrow sign flickers twice, rain falls, the man walks slowly away from camera, camera static".
- Remotion: sign text, a flicker on the beat of a dry line, cuts between signs.
- QA: figures turning toward camera (cut those shots), signs growing letters.

## 6. Prompt template

```
Flat mid-century illustration of a rainy [city] street at night in [year]. Glowing neon signs with blank shapes ([shapes]) in mustard and red, reflected in wet teal pavement. A single figure in [hat and coat] seen from behind [action], a solid ink shape. Flat colour, simple shapes, coarse halftone grain, strong vertical composition. Palette: ink #24211D, teal #2F7C78, mustard #D9A23A, cream #EFE3C8, red #C23A2B. No text, no letters, no faces.
```

## 7. When to use it

**Strong for:** crime, Prohibition, nightlife, cities, mystery, true-crime channels, music channels, moody Shorts and thumbnails.

**Weak for:** daytime, explaining, numbers. Pair with Isotype Ledger for the explaining.

## 8. Checklist

- [ ] Night, with light only from signs.
- [ ] Signs are wordless shapes.
- [ ] Figures are dark, from behind or far away.
- [ ] One accent sign.
- [ ] No text in the art.
